Why Specialty Pay Varies

Higher-acuity specialties such as ICU, CVICU, Cath Lab, and Labor & Delivery typically command significantly higher compensation due to severe staffing shortages, patient acuity requirements, certification requirements, and limited experienced candidate pools. Regional demand fluctuations, seasonal census changes, and hospital competition can also cause substantial weekly pay differences between contracts.
